Our Story

Custom Fabrications, Family Owned.

Signco Fabrications is a family-owned industrial sign company based in Elk Grove Village, Illinois, serving businesses across Chicagoland. We build large-scale industrial signage and LED signaling systems, and back every install with hands-on maintenance — because a sign is only as good as the company that stands behind it.

We're not a franchise and we don't outsource the work. From design through fabrication, installation, and service, the same team is accountable for the job start to finish.
