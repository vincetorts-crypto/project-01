How We Work

Our Process

Every project moves through the same five steps — so you always know what's next, and the work always gets done right.

01

Consultation & Site Survey

We start with a conversation about your goals, then visit the site in person to take measurements, check structural and electrical requirements, and confirm local permitting needs.

02

Design & Engineering

Our team produces custom drawings and engineering specs built to code for your sign type, size, and mounting conditions — reviewed with you before anything is built.

03

Fabrication

Your sign is fabricated in-house — structural framework, cabinetry, channel letters, and LED components — with quality checks at every stage.

04

Professional Installation

Our installation crews handle everything from footings and electrical hookup to crane-assisted placement for large-scale signage — safely, and on schedule.

05

Ongoing Maintenance

We don't disappear after install day. Signco offers routine inspections, LED and component repair, and rapid response for storm or damage repair.
